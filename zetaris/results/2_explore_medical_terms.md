## Q2. Translate the abbreviations from the heart-failure sample.

```sql
SELECT term, meaning, plain_language
FROM DAY_ONE.medical_terms
WHERE upper(term) IN ('BID', 'PRN', 'CHF', 'PO', 'SOB')
```

| term | meaning | plain_language |

| BID | twice daily | two times a day |
| PRN | as needed | only when you need it |
| PO | by mouth | swallow it |
| CHF | congestive heart failure | your heart does not pump as strongly as it should |
